How it works:

  • 14 teams go straight into the Round of 16 — type their names directly into the draw.
  • The other 4 teams play two play-in matches; the two winners fill the last 2 Round of 16 spots.
  • Every match is best of 3 games, standard padel/tennis scoring — 15, 30, 40, Game (win by two at deuce). Tap 1 / 2 / 3 for whoever wins that game; first to 2 games wins the match and advances automatically.
